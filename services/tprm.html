What Is Managed TPRM?

Managed Third-Party Risk Management is a continuous, end-to-end approach to identifying, assessing, monitoring, and mitigating vendor risk throughout the entire relationship lifecycle.

Centralized Vendor Inventory

Maintain a complete, accurate inventory of all vendors and third parties.

Risk-Based Tiering

Classify vendors based on criticality, data access, and impact.

Ongoing Risk Monitoring

Continuous oversight instead of one-time assessments.

Audit-Ready Evidence

Maintain documentation required for ISO, SOC, and customer reviews.
